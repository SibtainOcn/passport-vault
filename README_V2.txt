Passport Vault v2 - Automatic Excel Verification

New workflow:
1. Open Master Excel and upload your .xlsx/.csv master data.
2. Select the sheet/header and map Passport number plus the fields you want checked.
3. Save it as Active Master.
4. Upload passport scans/images/PDFs under Passports.
5. OCR runs locally and the passport is automatically compared with the active Master Excel.
6. Status becomes Approved, Needs Review, or Failed. Open a record to see Excel vs Passport OCR values side-by-side.

Important: automatic comparison checks data agreement only. It does not prove document authenticity.
